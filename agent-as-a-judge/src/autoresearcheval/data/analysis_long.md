## C. Execution & Implementation

11. **★ FE_GA is a pure branching ratio fixed by the barrier calibration — the "high selectivity" is a parameter consequence, not a finding.** In `fe_at_conditions`, `FE_GA = e_GA/(e_GA+e_FA)` with `e_GA=2·K_DES_GA·θ_GA` and `e_FA=2·K_DES_FA·θ_FA+2·k_cc·θ_EG`. The C₂/C₁ branch happens at the `*EG` node: `r_deh1=k_deh1·θ_EG` against `r_cc=k_cc·θ_EG`, so the ratio is just `k_deh1/k_cc`. Since `Ea0(deh1)=0.60` ≪ `Ea0(cc)=0.85`, and at U=1.4 the deh1 barrier is `0.60+0.05−0.7=−0.05` → clamped to the **0.05 eV floor** while the cc barrier is `0.85−0.155−0.42≈0.275`, we get `k_deh1/k_cc = exp((0.275−0.05)/0.0257) ≈ e^8.75 ≈ 6300×`.
